# 🔥 Is Not a Lead. These 3 Comments Are. 💬

Every time you post, your comment section quietly runs a lead-gen campaign — whether you're watching or not.

The problem? Most businesses treat every reaction the same. They chase hearts and leave money sitting in the replies.

Here's how to read your comment section like a salesperson 👇

1️⃣ **"Price?" / "How much?"** — They're not haggling. They've already decided they want it and are checking if it fits. That's a hand raised. Reply in minutes, not days.

2️⃣ **"Can you do this for ___?"** — That's not a question, that's a brief. They're picturing their project with you in it. Answer with one question that moves it into the DMs.

3️⃣ **A tag + "we need this"** — Two people are interested and one of them has budget. That tag is a warm intro you didn't pay for.

Meanwhile the 🔥 emojis are applause. Feels great. Buys nothing.

Here's the catch nobody mentions: buying signals don't keep business hours. The "price?" comment at 11pm is usually cold by morning — scrolled past, buried, or answered by a competitor who was awake. 🕒

That's exactly the gap we build for: content that earns the attention, and an AI employee that watches the comment section, flags the buying signals, and makes sure no "how much?" ever waits until sunrise. 🤖

## Key Takeaways
- "Price?" isn't a haggling question — it's a buying signal wearing a disguise
- Applause (🔥) feels good but doesn't convert; specific questions do
- Speed matters as much as skill: leads expire overnight
