# Your Error Log Is Your Automation Roadmap

The most valuable document in your business isn't your strategy deck. It's the list of things that went wrong this month.

Most businesses treat recurring mistakes as people problems. "Be more careful." "Double-check next time." But the same error happening twice isn't carelessness — it's a process asking to be automated.

Three signals I look for:

→ The same mistake appears more than once a quarter
→ Errors cluster at handoffs between people or tools
→ Things break exactly when everyone is busiest

If a mistake follows a pattern, a system can catch it — or prevent it entirely. That's where AI employees earn their keep: they don't get tired at 4pm on a Friday.

## Key Takeaways
- Recurring errors are process problems, not people problems
- Patterns in mistakes point directly at what to automate first
- Automation is quality control that never has an off day
