# 2D or 3D? Pick the Wrong One and Your Budget Works Against You 🎬

Most people choose animation style by asking "what looks more impressive?" Wrong question. The right question is: **what does this piece of content need to DO?**

Here's the cheat sheet we use on every project:

**Go 3D when you need:** product reveals, depth and realism, that premium brand moment people screenshot and share. 3D sells *quality*.

**Go 2D / motion graphics when you need:** fast-turnaround reels, explainers, educational content, personality-driven storytelling. 2D sells *clarity*.

The style isn't a taste decision — it's a strategy decision. Match it to the job and even a modest budget looks premium. Mismatch it and even a big budget looks off.

## Key Takeaways
- 3D = depth, realism, premium feel. Best for products and brand moments.
- 2D = speed, clarity, personality. Best for explainers and reels.
- Start with the action you want the viewer to take — then choose the visuals.
