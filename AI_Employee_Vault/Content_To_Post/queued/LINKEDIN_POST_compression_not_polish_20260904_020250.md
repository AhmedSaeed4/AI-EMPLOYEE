# Your Product Video Isn't Competing With Other Videos. It's Competing With Confusion.

Every lost sale had a moment in it: the prospect looked at your offer and didn't understand it fast enough. They didn't say no. They said "I'll figure it out later."

Later never comes.

That's the real job of animation. Not polish — compression.

Text asks a prospect to assemble your product in their head. A 15-second video that shows the mechanism does the assembling for them. The difference isn't aesthetics. It's seconds, and seconds are the scarcest thing your buyer owns.

Three rules we build by:

**1. Show the mechanism, not the claim.** "Saves you hours" is a claim anyone can make. A shot of the report building itself is evidence.

**2. One idea per video.** A video that explains everything explains nothing. The second idea is a second video — cheaper than you'd think.

**3. Assume the sound is off.** Most business videos get watched muted the first time. If it only works with audio, it only works once.

You're not paying for render time. You're paying for the shortest distance between "what is this?" and "I need this."
