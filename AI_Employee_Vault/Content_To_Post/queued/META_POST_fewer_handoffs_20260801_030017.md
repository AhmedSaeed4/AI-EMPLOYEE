# You Don't Need More Hours. You Need Fewer Handoffs. 🔄

You don't have a time problem. You have a handoff problem.

Every time you copy details from an email into a spreadsheet… switch between three apps to publish one post… or sit idle waiting on someone else before you can move — that's a handoff. And every handoff quietly leaks time, energy, and money. 💸

Here's what nobody tells you: most "busy" work isn't the tasks themselves. It's the *gaps* between them. The copying. The pasting. The "where was I again?" The mental reload every time you switch context.

That friction is invisible on your to-do list, but it's the real reason your day disappears. 🕳️

This is exactly where AI agents change the game. Instead of 5 disconnected tools that need YOU to glue them together, a Digital FTE runs the whole chain start-to-finish — while you focus on the work that actually moves the needle.
