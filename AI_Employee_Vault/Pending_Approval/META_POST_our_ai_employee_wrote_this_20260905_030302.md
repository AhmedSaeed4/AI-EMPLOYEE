# We Build AI Employees. One of Them Drafted This Post. 🤖

Before we ever sell a digital employee, it has to earn its keep inside our own business first. Ours triages the inbox, organizes files, drafts social content (including this post 📝), tracks invoices, and logs its own errors when something breaks.

The honest part? It doesn't do anything sensitive on its own. Money, messages, posts — everything lands in a review queue and a human presses the button. That's not a limitation. That's the design. Speed on the busywork. Human judgment on the decisions that matter.

And that's our quality bar: if a capability can't survive our own Monday mornings, we don't ship it to yours.

## Key Takeaways
- We run what we sell — our own AI employees handle inbox, files, invoicing, and content drafts
- Nothing sensitive moves without human approval; that's the design, not a restriction
- Every feature gets tested on our business before it ever touches yours
