# Your prices are pinned to your hours. That's why you're tired *and* undercharging.

Most service pricing works the same way: estimate the hours, add a margin, send the invoice.

So earning more always means being present more. Your calendar is the proof.

Here's the trap nobody names. The repeatable 80% — the replies, the follow-ups, the quotes, the chasing — is priced like commodity time. It's not what makes you valuable. It's what eats the hours you'd otherwise spend on the work that is.

Every hour you spend on it is an hour you can't sell at premium rates, because you've already spent it on something a client would never pay top dollar for.

Automation isn't a productivity upgrade. It's a pricing unlock. When a Digital FTE carries that repeatable 80% inside guardrails you set once, you stop trading hours for the commodity layer — and you finally have the capacity to price and deliver the premium layer properly.

You stop earning more by working more. You earn more by removing yourself from the part that doesn't need you.

## Key Takeaways
- Hour-based service pricing keeps you pinned to manual speed — earning more always means working more
- The repeatable 80% is priced like commodity time and quietly eats the hours that should go to high-margin work
- Automation is a pricing unlock, not a productivity hack — a Digital FTE removes you from the commodity layer so you scale margin, not hours

If you could stop doing one repeatable task today without a client ever noticing, what would it be? Drop it below — I'll tell you if it's the first thing an AI employee should take off your plate. 👇
