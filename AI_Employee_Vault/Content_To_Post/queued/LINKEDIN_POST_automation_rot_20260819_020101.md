# Your Automations Are Decaying Right Now. You Just Haven't Noticed.

Every workflow you build is a bet that nothing around it changes.

An email template gets redesigned. A folder gets renamed. An API shifts one field. Your automation doesn't crash — it quietly does the wrong thing. Every single day.

The scariest failures aren't the loud ones. They're silent: the lead alert that stopped firing weeks ago, the data sync writing to the wrong place, the follow-up that never went out.

Here's the maintenance loop we run on every AI employee:

**1. Alert on silence.** If a workflow that runs daily hasn't logged anything in 24 hours, that IS the error. Absence is a signal.

**2. The 15-minute weekly audit.** What ran? What skipped? What needed a human? Fifteen minutes catches what three months of drift destroys.

**3. Map the dependencies.** Before changing any process, know what automations lean on it. Folders, formats, rules — they're all load-bearing.

Automation isn't a project with a ship date. It's an employee that needs managing.

Have you ever discovered an automation that had been broken for weeks? How did you catch it? 👇

## Key Takeaways
- Automations fail silently more often than loudly
- No logs in 24h = your first error signal
- A 15-minute weekly audit prevents months of drift
