# Unpopular Opinion: AI Employees Aren't Taking Jobs. They're Taking Busywork.

Every week I hear the same fear: "AI is coming for our jobs."

After building Digital FTEs — AI employees that handle real workflows — here's what I've actually seen:

**Myth 1: AI replaces people.**
Reality: it replaces the 15 hours/week your team loses to inbox triage, data entry, and follow-ups. The people stay. The busywork goes.

**Myth 2: AI employees are just chatbots.**
Reality: a real Digital FTE watches your channels, drafts responses, routes approvals, and flags anything sensitive for a human. It works 24/7 — and knows when to ask.

**Myth 3: Set it and forget it.**
Reality: an AI employee is only as good as the rules you give it. We spend more time on guardrails than on the automation itself.

The businesses winning with AI aren't replacing their teams. They're giving every person an extra pair of hands that never sleeps.

## Key Takeaways
- AI employees eliminate busywork, not people
- Good Digital FTEs escalate sensitive work to humans
