# Most Animation Projects Don't Fail at the Render Stage. They Fail in the Brief.

Whether it's a 3D product reel, 2D VFX, or a short-form content series — the projects that ship smoothly and the ones that stall usually differ on one thing: what happened before a single frame was made.

If you're thinking about hiring an animator, get these 5 things ready first:

**1. The one sentence.** What should someone know, feel, or do after watching? If you can't write it down, no amount of motion will fix it.

**2. Where it lives.** A reel cut for Instagram isn't one for your sales deck. Platform decides length, pacing, even aspect ratio — decide before you start, not after.

**3. What "good" looks like.** Send 2–3 reference videos you love (and one you hate). This single step kills more revision rounds than anything else.

**4. Your real deadline.** Not the hopeful one — the one where this must be live. Honest timelines need honest inputs.

**5. Who says yes.** One decision-maker for feedback. Five sets of notes equals five different videos.

For context: our delivery windows run from 24 hours to 30 days depending on scope, because complexity varies. If a studio promises fixed 24-hour turnaround on everything, something is being rushed — probably your project.

## Key Takeaways
- Most animation projects fail at the brief stage, not the production stage
- One sentence of intent beats pages of vague direction
- Reference videos and a single decision-maker cut revision cycles dramatically
