# Unpopular Opinion: "24-Hour Delivery" Is a Red Flag, Not a Flex 🚩

Anyone who's commissioned a 3D animation knows the drill. You collect quotes and one studio promises your custom 3D reel "tomorrow" — another asks for two weeks.

Here's the uncomfortable truth: the 24-hour quote isn't faster. It's either a template with your logo swapped in, or a project about to miss every deadline after the first round of feedback. ⏳

Real animation has steps that can't be compressed — concept, modeling, texturing, lighting, render passes, revisions. Rush any one of them, and it shows up on screen. Your audience can't name what's wrong, but they feel it and scroll past.

That's why our timelines range from 24 hours to 30 days: we scope honestly based on complexity, not on whatever sounds best in a DM. ✅

Some content genuinely is a same-day job. A full 3D product animation isn't. We'd rather tell you that up front than send you "final_final_v7.mp4" three weeks late.

## Key Takeaways
- 🎨 Fast-delivery promises often mean templates, not custom work
- ⚙️ Real timelines reflect real production steps — scope drives speed
- 🤝 Honest scoping builds trust that flashy quotes can't buy
