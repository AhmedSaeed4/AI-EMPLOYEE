# 15 Seconds of Animation. Days of Work. Here's Why. 🎬

From the outside it looks simple: "make it move, make it look cool, done."

From the inside, here's what stands between your brief and the reel that stops the scroll 👇

**1. The brief + style frames.** Before anything moves, we lock the LOOK. A couple of still frames that decide your colors, lighting, and mood. Get this right and everything after goes fast. Skip it and you pay for it in revisions later.

**2. Blocking.** The rough version — simple shapes, simple motion. This is where timing gets locked. If your product reveal belongs at second 3 instead of second 5, it gets caught here, while it's cheap to change.

**3. Lighting, materials, detail.** This is where "that looks expensive" actually comes from — how light behaves on your surface, reflections, depth. Hours of tuning per shot.

**4. The render.** The computer takes over, and it isn't instant. Every second of final-quality 3D is dozens of still frames. A 15-second reel can be hundreds of frames, each one taking minutes to render.

**5. Polish + delivery.** Sound, final timing, platform-specific cuts — a reel built for Instagram isn't automatically the right cut for Facebook.

Here's the honest part: that's why we don't promise fixed "24-hour delivery" on everything. Simple 2D can genuinely be same-day. A detailed 3D product reel can take weeks — because the scope demands it, not because anyone's slow.

The timeline should match the work. 🤝
