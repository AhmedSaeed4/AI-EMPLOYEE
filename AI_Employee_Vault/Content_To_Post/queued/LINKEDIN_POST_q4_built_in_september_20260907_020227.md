# Q4 Is Decided in September. By November, It's Over.

Every October the same scene: orders pile up, the inbox floods — and the owner realizes they need more capacity.

Problem: capacity isn't a switch.

An AI employee needs scoping, testing, and earned trust before real work. Q4 campaign content needs briefing and production before the season starts. Both take weeks. October is when they must be running — not when you start building.

The businesses that own Q4 aren't thinking about Q4 in November. They're running systems they built in September.

One rule we run by: **build in the quiet, run in the loud.** Automating mid-season means debugging while driving. Build now — while you can still test — and it runs itself when you have no hours left.

Three things to do in the next 30 days:

1. List what breaks at 2x volume — those are Q4 failures waiting to happen
2. Automate ONE of them while there's time to test
3. Get Q4 visual content into production — studios free now are booked by November

## Key Takeaways
- Capacity takes weeks to build; demand arrives on a date
- Never deploy untested automation in peak season
- Build in the quiet, run in the loud
