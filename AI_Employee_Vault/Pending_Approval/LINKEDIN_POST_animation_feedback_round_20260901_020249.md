# The Most Expensive Words in Animation Don't Appear on the Quote

The most expensive words in an animation project don't appear on the quote. They show up in the feedback round: "Can you make it pop?"

Every studio prices revisions. Almost nobody budgets for decoding them. "Make it pop" might mean brighter color, faster pacing, punchier sound design — or a different concept entirely. There's only one way to find out, and it costs a revision round either way.

The clients with the fastest turnarounds aren't the easiest ones. They're the most specific:

→ "The logo reveal at 0:07 drags" — not "the intro feels off"
→ "Can we see it in our brand blue?" — not "different colors"
→ Timestamps over vibes, every time

Same number of revision rounds in your quote. Radically different results inside them.

## Key Takeaways
- Vague feedback isn't faster. It just moves the cost onto your timeline.
- Anchor every note to a timestamp.
- Can't name what's wrong? Say what it's doing to you. "It feels slow" is usable. "It needs something" isn't.
