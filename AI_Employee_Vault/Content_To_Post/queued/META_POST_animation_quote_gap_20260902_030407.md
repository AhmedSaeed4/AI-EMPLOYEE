# Two Creators. The "Same" Video. Quotes 10x Apart. 🎬

Neither studio is lying. They're just not quoting the same thing.

When a quote feels too high — or too good to be true — it usually comes down to four things:

1️⃣ **Complexity** — 2D motion graphics and full 3D are different crafts. Modeling, lighting, and rendering take real compute and real time.

2️⃣ **The invisible pipeline** — the brief, the style frames, the revision rounds you never see. That's where the quality actually gets made. Cut them and you feel it on screen.

3️⃣ **Turnaround** — a flexible timeline is cheaper than a rush job. Always.

4️⃣ **Usage** — one reel for your feed is a different asset than a video you're running ads on for a year.

So before you compare quotes, compare what's *inside* them: How many revisions? Are style frames included? Do you get the source files?

The expensive quote is often just the honest one. 💡

## Key Takeaways
- 2D motion graphics and full 3D are priced differently for a reason
- Briefs, style frames, and revisions are where quality is made — cutting them cuts quality
- Ask what's included: revision rounds, source files, usage rights
