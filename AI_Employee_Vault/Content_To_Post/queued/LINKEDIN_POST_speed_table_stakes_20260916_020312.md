# Unpopular Prediction: By 2027, "We Reply in Minutes" Won't Sell Anything

Right now, it's the strongest line in small business: reply to leads fast and you win.

Two years from now, it's plumbing.

Because everyone is buying the same thing — an AI that answers instantly. When every inbox replies in 4 minutes, speed stops being a differentiator. It becomes table stakes, like having a website.

The uncomfortable part: most businesses are spending their whole automation budget on the layer that stops differentiating first.

What survives the squeeze:

→ **Judgment in the first human reply.** Speed gets you considered. The quality of the answer gets you chosen.

→ **Proof you can show.** When everyone's fast, the work does the selling — motion, craft, results a summary can't fake.

→ **Follow-through after the fast reply.** Onboarding, delivery, the details nobody automated.

We build the fast layer — Digital FTEs that triage, follow up, and never sleep. We just don't pretend it's the moat. It's the entry ticket. The moat is the craft you point it at.

Fast is becoming free. What you do with the reclaimed hours isn't.
