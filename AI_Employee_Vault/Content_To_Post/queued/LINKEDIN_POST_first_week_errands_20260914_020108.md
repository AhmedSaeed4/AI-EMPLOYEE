# Stop Planning Your AI Employee. Give It Errands.

Most businesses stall on AI automation because they're waiting for the perfect grand strategy. Meanwhile, the businesses actually getting results started with something embarrassingly small: a week of errands.

Here are 5 we hand over first:

1. **Inbox triage** — Sorting, flagging, and drafting replies before a human touches anything
2. **Invoice and payment tracking** — Chasing status, not moving money
3. **File organization** — Renaming, filing, and finding the "final_v9" mess
4. **Task routing** — Turning messy requests into clean, assigned to-dos
5. **Daily reports** — Summarizing what happened so nobody has to ask

Notice what's missing: judgment calls, client relationships, money movement. Those stay human — at first.

The pattern? Every errand is repetitive, rule-based, and verifiable. Nail those, and trust compounds. By week three, you're delegating things that actually feel like work.

## Key Takeaways
- Start with errands, not strategy — momentum beats planning
- Repetitive + rule-based + verifiable = your first delegation list
- Trust is built in small, boring wins before big ones
