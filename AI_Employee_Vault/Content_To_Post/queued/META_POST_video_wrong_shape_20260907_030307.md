# Your Video Isn't Bad. It's the Wrong Shape. 📐

We review a lot of business videos that "just don't perform" — and half the time the animation, edit, and message are fine. The video was simply delivered in the wrong shape for where it lives.

The three shapes that matter:

1️⃣ **9:16 vertical** — Reels, Stories, Shorts. Full-screen. The ONLY shape where you own the entire phone instead of a window inside it.

2️⃣ **1:1 square** — Feed posts. Safe everywhere, dominant nowhere. The compromise shape.

3️⃣ **16:9 widescreen** — YouTube, your website, sales decks. Reads as "a real video" — and gets letterboxed into a thin strip the moment you post it as a Reel.

Here's the trap: one export, posted everywhere. The widescreen cut shrinks to a third of the screen on Reels. The vertical cut gets cropped in feed previews — and your caption text gets clipped.

And the detail almost everyone misses: **safe zones**. On Reels, the username, caption, and buttons cover the bottom quarter and the right edge. If your logo or key message sits there, it's hidden behind the app's own UI. 🫥

We never deliver a single file. Same 15 seconds of work, cut for each platform it will live on — because the render is only half the job.

## Key Takeaways
- "Underperforming video" is often just video in the wrong format
- Vertical (9:16) is the only shape that owns the full screen — use it where you can
- Keep text and logos inside the safe zone, or the app's UI buries them
