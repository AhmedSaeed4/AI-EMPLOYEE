# Before You Hire Anyone to Automate Your Business, Ask These 5 Questions

The AI automation space is full of impressive demos and quiet disappearances. Most buyers learn the difference three months after the invoice.

Ask these before you sign:

**1. "Where does it stop without me?"** If they can't point to exact guardrails — what it's never allowed to do without human approval — they've built a demo, not a system.

**2. "What happens when it breaks?"** Every automation decays. APIs change, formats shift. If maintenance isn't in scope, you're pre-ordering surprise invoices.

**3. "Who owns the system?"** If the docs, code, and credentials don't land in your hands, you don't own an asset. You're renting your own operations.

**4. "What did you say no to recently?"** Honest scoping leaves a trail. A yes to everything is how you end up with a 24-hour promise on a 30-day project.

**5. "Can I see it on messy, real data?"** Demos are staged. Workflows aren't. Watch how it handles the weird input — that's your actual experience.

Vendors who clear all five are rare. Those are the ones worth paying.

## Key Takeaways
- Guardrails tell you more than feature lists
- Maintenance and ownership are where hidden costs hide
- Honest scoping now beats apology emails later
