# This Post Was Drafted by My AI Employee. That's the Whole Pitch.

I could tell you AI employees work. Or I could show you the receipt.

Every post on this page — including this one — started in our AI employee's queue. It read our goals, checked past posts so it wouldn't repeat itself, then filed this draft for review.

Nothing goes out until a human approves it. That's not a limitation — that's the design.

Three things building our own taught us:

**1. The value isn't the drafting — it's the memory.** The system knows what we posted yesterday, so it won't post it again tomorrow. Consistency at scale is the product, not the perk.

**2. Guardrails ARE the feature.** Ours can't send money or publish anything without explicit sign-off. Businesses don't fear AI capability — they fear AI autonomy. Remove that fear and adoption gets easy.

**3. Run it on yourself first.** We automated our own business before offering to automate yours. Every edge case hit us first. So when we scope your Digital FTE, we're describing a system we live inside — not one we read about.

If you're evaluating an AI employee, ask any vendor one question: "Do you run yours?"
