# We Build AI Employees for a Living. Here's What We'll Never Let Ours Do.

The most important line in a Digital FTE's job description isn't what it should do. It's what it's never allowed to do.

We spend more time on the "never" list than the task list — because trust isn't built by automation doing more. It's built by knowing exactly where it stops.

Three things that stay human, every time:

**1. Money moves.** An AI can draft the invoice, flag the overdue account, prep the payment run. It doesn't press send. Anything touching cash gets a human approval first.

**2. Commitments with your name on them.** Deadlines, pricing, scope. "Can you deliver by Friday?" gets drafted — never answered. The AI prepares the answer; you make the promise.

**3. Public replies.** Comments, DMs, reviews. Drafted, approved, then posted. You loosen the leash one workflow at a time.

Here's the counterintuitive part: the guardrails are why the automation works. When your AI knows exactly where it stops, you stop checking everything it does. You review 5% instead of supervising 100%.

Full autonomy isn't the goal. The right autonomy is.

## Key Takeaways
- Write the "never" list before the task list
- Money, commitments, and public voice stay human
- Guardrails are what make autonomy trustworthy
