# Your Animation Isn't Late Because Rendering Takes Long. 🕐

Here's the part nobody puts on a timeline: most video projects don't stall in production. They stall in the gap between "here's draft 1" and the day someone finally replies.

We've watched a 3-day animation sit for 4 days waiting on feedback — then get called "late." The render was never the bottleneck. Clarity was.

Three things that make feedback rounds painless 👇

1️⃣ **One document, not fourteen messages.** "Make it punchier" in one chat and "make it longer" in another sends us in two directions at once. Consolidate before you send — it literally cuts a full round.

2️⃣ **Decide who decides.** If a third person sees the draft at round 2 and has notes, that's a new round — and it was predictable on day one. One decision-maker, everyone else consulted before we start.

3️⃣ **React to the style frame, not the final render.** A change at the storyboard stage costs minutes. The same change after rendering costs days. We'd always rather tell you that upfront than bill you for it later.

This is exactly why we quote timelines based on project scope — not a fake "24-hour delivery" promise. The honest number includes the part you control. 🤝

## Key Takeaways
- Feedback speed is part of your delivery date
- One consolidated feedback document per round
- Approve the direction early — changes get more expensive after the render
