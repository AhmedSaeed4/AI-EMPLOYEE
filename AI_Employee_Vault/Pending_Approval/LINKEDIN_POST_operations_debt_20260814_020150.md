# Your business is carrying debt you'll never see on a balance sheet.

Every "I'll just do it manually for now." Every spreadsheet built as a quick fix. Every follow-up you run from memory. Every process that lives inside one person's head.

That's operations debt — the business version of technical debt. And it works exactly like the financial kind: cheap to take on, brutal to carry.

You borrow time today by skipping the system. Then you pay interest every single day in your own hours.

The danger isn't any single workaround — each one looks harmless on its own. It's that they compound. A new client adds a new manual step. A new channel adds a new thing to remember. Give it a year and half your week is spent servicing the shortcuts you took to save time.

Here's the reframe that changes it: a Digital FTE doesn't just do the work. It pays down the debt. Every task you hand off is a workaround you stop paying interest on — permanently.

You keep servicing the daily tax. Or you build the system once and own the asset.

## Key Takeaways
- Manual workarounds aren't free — they're loans against your future time, and they compound
- Operations debt never shows up on financials, but it fills your calendar
- Every task you automate pays down debt for good instead of just covering the interest
