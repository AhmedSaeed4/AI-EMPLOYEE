# Never Approve an Animation You Haven't Seen as a Still

Here's the step most clients never hear about — and why our projects don't blow up in week three.

Before any animation starts, we produce a **style frame**: one fully-rendered still of your finished video. Lighting, color, texture, mood. Everything except motion.

Why bother?

Because motion hides problems. A moving shot is exciting, and excitement makes people approve what they should question. A still has nowhere to hide: wrong palette, flat lighting, off-brand mood — obvious in one frame, invisible until render day.

At style frame stage, that conversation costs hours. At final render, it costs the project.

So when comparing studios, ask one question: **"Will I see and approve a still frame before you animate?"**

- If yes — they front-load clarity instead of billing for surprise.
- If they hesitate — budget for revisions you didn't plan for.

## Key Takeaways
- A style frame locks the look before motion begins
- Motion persuades — the worst moment for visual decisions
- The still-frame question separates pros from hope-and-bill shops
