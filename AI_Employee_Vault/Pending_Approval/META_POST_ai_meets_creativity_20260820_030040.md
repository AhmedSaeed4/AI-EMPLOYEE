# Behind the Scenes: What Happens When AI Meets Creativity 🎬

Everyone talks about AI replacing creative work.

Here's what we actually see behind the scenes: AI doesn't make the art. It clears the runway so the art can take off. ✈️

A typical project in our studio runs on two tracks:

🤖 **Track 1 — The AI side:** A digital assistant sorts incoming briefs, drafts project timelines, flags what the client needs to approve, and keeps everyone updated. No chasing, no spreadsheet chaos.

🎨 **Track 2 — The human side:** Our artists focus purely on the 3D animation, the VFX, the storytelling — the stuff machines can't feel.

The result? Projects that used to drown in admin now move at the speed of the creative work itself.

The same applies to your business, even if you've never touched animation:

👉 If a task is repetitive, predictable, and eats your hours — that's AI territory.
👉 If a task needs taste, judgment, or emotion — that's yours.

The mistake isn't using AI. The mistake is using it on the wrong side of that line.

## Key Takeaways
- AI handles the repetitive pipeline; humans handle taste and judgment
- Less admin = faster creative delivery (and happier clients)
- The AI/creativity "rivalry" is a myth — they're collaborators

Which side of the line does YOUR workday sit on? Share one task you'd hand to AI tomorrow 👇
