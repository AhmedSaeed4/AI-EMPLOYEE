# 5 Questions to Ask Before You Hire an Automation Partner

The AI space is full of impressive demos and quiet disappearances. Most buyers learn the difference three months after the invoice.

Ask these before you sign:

**1. "Where does it stop without me?"** No exact guardrails — nothing it's barred from doing without approval — means they built a demo, not a system.

**2. "What happens when it breaks?"** Every automation decays. If maintenance isn't in scope, you're pre-ordering surprise invoices.

**3. "Who owns the system?"** If docs, code, and credentials don't land in your hands, you're renting your own operations.

**4. "What did you say no to recently?"** Honest scoping leaves a trail. A yes to everything is how you get a 24-hour promise on a 30-day project.

**5. "Can I see it on messy, real data?"** Demos are staged. Workflows aren't. How it handles the weird input is your real experience.

Vendors who clear all five are rare. Those are the ones worth paying.

## Key Takeaways
- Guardrails tell you more than feature lists
- Maintenance and ownership hide the real costs
- Honest scoping beats apology emails
