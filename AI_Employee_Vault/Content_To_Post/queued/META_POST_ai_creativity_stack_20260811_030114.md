Most businesses treat AI and creative work like they're on opposite teams.

The "tech" side automates everything. The "creative" side guards their craft from the machines. And never the two shall meet. 🙅‍♂️

Here's what I've learned building both under one roof:

The businesses pulling ahead right now aren't choosing sides. They're **stacking** AI and creativity into a single workflow — and the combination is doing things neither could do alone.

🤖 The AI handles the repetitive, time-sensitive work: lead follow-ups, inbox triage, data processing, that 2am customer question nobody's awake to answer.

Separately, each is powerful. Together, they create a compounding advantage — faster response **AND** a stronger first impression.
