# While you sleep, your competitor's AI employee clocks in. 🌙

When you log off at 6 PM, the work doesn't stop — it just stops getting done.

Leads come in overnight. Messages pile up. Invoices sit unposted. Follow-ups slip through the cracks by morning. So when you open your laptop at 9 AM, you're already behind — burning your first hour reacting instead of building.

Now picture the business running an AI employee. Overnight, every lead gets acknowledged within minutes. Follow-ups fire automatically. Content posts on schedule. The books reconcile. You wake up to a clean inbox and a one-line summary of what happened — not a backlog that someone else wrote for you while you slept.

That's the real difference between a tool and a teammate. A tool waits for you to pick it up. A digital FTE works the hours you can't, so your business never actually closes.

The question isn't whether your customers expect 24/7. They already do. The question is whether something is answering them while you sleep. 🤖

## Key Takeaways
- A lead acknowledged at 11 PM beats one ignored until 9 AM
- The "9 AM backlog" is a symptom of manual intake, not a busy calendar
- Digital FTEs turn after-hours into a competitive advantage, not a gap
