# Your Video Doesn't Lose Viewers at 0:30. It Loses Them at 0:03.

We produce short-form animation for businesses, and the difference between a reel that converts and one that disappears is almost never the editing. It's what happens in the first three seconds.

Three things that decide it:

**1. Motion in frame one.** A static logo or someone walking into position reads as "ad." The eye bails before your message even loads.

**2. Show the payoff first.** Lead with the finished result — the product spinning, the dashboard updating — then explain how you got there. Curiosity works backwards.

**3. Cut on action, never after it.** Every frame where nothing moves is an exit door. If a shot ends at the peak of motion, the next one starts with momentum already earned.

None of this requires a bigger budget. It requires deciding that the first three seconds are the project — not the intro to it.
